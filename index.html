Where to start

Two small moves, and every venture after this one gets cheaper

Ingenia trades expertise for equity and wins on reusable IP. That logic ought to apply to the studio's own operations before it applies to anything ambitious. These two are the lowest-cost, fastest-moving places to test it: no new architecture, no data model to design first, and both address something your team is already working around this month.

Seam one - people

Coordination across organisations

Studio staff, eight portfolio companies, a Silicon Valley strategy layer, an India-based execution team and outside delivery partners. None of them sit inside one company boundary, and the work moves between them every day.

Why it is low-hanging: you already pay for Slack and the adoption is done. This is a tier change, not a new tool and not a new habit.
Seam two - systems

Connection across data

Each venture arrives with its own production databases, its own drivers and its own credentials. Your engineers are working through exactly this on the current Tableau build, one database at a time.

Why it is low-hanging: the work is happening anyway. The only question is whether it becomes an asset or evaporates.
Seam one

Slack: one workspace, a dozen companies

Slack Pro is doing a good job of what it is built for: one team, in one workspace. Ingenia is not one team. It is a studio, eight ventures, two continents and a set of outside partners, and the plan underneath it has no concept of that structure. Enterprise+ is the first tier that does.

Your own instinct, at the end of our last working session, was to set up a shared channel so the studio, our team and your delivery partner could stay connected and share collateral in one place.

That instinct is right, and Pro will let you do it once. The question this page asks is what happens when it repeats across a dozen ventures, each with its own people, partners and obligations.

Next steps

Two small decisions, in the order they get easier

We have deliberately kept this to the two things that ask least of you. Neither needs a new architecture, a data model, or a long evaluation, and both touch work already on your team's desk.

First

Move Slack to Enterprise+

Stand up an organisation rather than a workspace, with residency chosen deliberately at creation and a workspace ready for each venture as it spins up.

Lowest effort. A tier change on a tool you already run, no engineering time.
Second

Scope MuleSoft against the live build

Take the connections your team is working on right now and cost out what it takes to make them reusable assets rather than one-off work. Use the project already underway as the test case.

Moderate effort. No new project required.
Then

Judge it on the second venture

If the next venture's integration and onboarding is meaningfully cheaper than this one, the reuse thesis holds and we extend it. If not, you will have spent very little finding out.

A decision on evidence rather than argument.
